Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ 💡

Got questions? We've got answers. If yours isn't here, reach out to the team!

What time should I arrive on my first day?+
Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled start time. Your supervisor or the TA team will confirm your exact start time beforehand. If you haven't received confirmation, contact Nanda Putri via WhatsApp.
What should I wear on my first day?+
Wear neat, professional attire on your first day. Your WM Center uniform will be given during onboarding (except for Back Office positions). Once you receive your uniform, please wear it on working days.
How do I set up my Talenta HRIS account?+
Follow the step-by-step tutorial at bit.ly/TutorialTalentaWMCenter. If you encounter any issues, contact Fionna Uguy from the HR team.
When will I receive my first paycheck?+
Salary is paid monthly. The exact date will be communicated by the Finance team. Make sure your bank account information is submitted correctly through the Employee Data Form. For questions, contact Deogy Ahmad.
What documents do I need to submit?+
You need to submit: KTP, KK (Family Card), recent non-formal photo, and diploma (for Physiotherapist & Front Office positions). Upload these through the Document Upload form on the Pre-Onboarding page. Also fill in the Personal Data Form completely.
Who do I contact if I have questions not listed here?+
Reach out to the Recruitment & Onboarding team — Nanda Putri is your go-to person for any onboarding-related questions. You can also check the "Who to Go To" section above for other topics.
How do I claim reimbursements?+
Reimbursement claims are processed through the Finance team. Contact Deogy Ahmad via WhatsApp and provide your receipts and claim details. Make sure to submit claims within the allowed timeframe as communicated by your supervisor.
What group chats should I be added to?+
You should be added to: your Division/Branch group, the Internal Info Group (WM Office or WM Branch), and the Full Team group. If you haven't been added, contact Nanda Putri or your direct supervisor.
